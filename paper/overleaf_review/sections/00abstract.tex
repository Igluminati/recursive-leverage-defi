This project empirically studies recursive leverage strategies among large holders of yield-bearing stablecoins on Ethereum mainnet. We develop a proof-of-concept bot demonstrating looping mechanics on Morpho Blue, build a data pipeline to identify whale addresses across Aave v3, Morpho Blue, and analyse strategy archetypes using machine learning. Our contributions include a working implementation of flashloan-based and iterative looping strategies, a comprehensive dataset of whale activity, and insights into risk management behaviours that inform DeFi protocol design and systemic risk assessment.
